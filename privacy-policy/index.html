Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Data Collection

Thunderkeg does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, account systems, or remote video processing services.

Videos And Files

Videos you select are used only for local compression on your device. Compressed outputs are written to the destination you choose or to the app's local storage area, depending on platform behavior.

Thunderkeg does not upload your videos to a server.

Network Access

Thunderkeg does not require network access for compression.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support link listed with the app store listing or visit Thunderkeg Support.
